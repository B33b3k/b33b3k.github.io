Privacy Policy

Scope

Card Table is a solo card-game app operated by Bibek Adhikari. You do not need to create an account. The app does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. This policy covers the current app and this page.

Data stored on your device

Your selected avatar, virtual-chip balance, table settings and game statistics are stored on your device. They are not sent to a Card Table server. The app has no ads, in-app purchases or real-money payments. Virtual chips have no cash value.

Backups and platform services

This build does not use analytics, push notifications or over-the-air updates. Android may include app data in your device backup depending on your system settings. Google Play's distribution and protection features are governed by Google's own privacy practices.

Deleting your data

You can remove locally stored app data using Android Settings, then Apps, Card Table, Storage, Clear data. This resets your saved preferences and progress. There is no online Card Table account to delete.

Updates and contact

For privacy questions, contact: contact@bibekadhikari18.com.np. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
